# Wildfire Rescue Queue — intake agent

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Resident-facing: request rescue, tell us what you see (text, voice, photos, video), check your queue status, mark safe.

**Try it in ASI:One:** "I need help, there's a fire near me" → fill the form → "flames behind the house, road blocked by a fallen tree" → "status" → "I'm safe".

Requests rescue for residents threatened by a wildfire and reads what they report (fire nearby, roads blocked, people hurt or trapped) into structured claims for the incident commander, shows their live queue position, assigned unit, ETA and the reason, and deletes their location when they are safe or consent expires. Simulated demo — not a replacement for 911.

Part of a multi-agent system coordinating through a shared SpacetimeDB world state.
