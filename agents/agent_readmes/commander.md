ASI:One incident-commander console: situation, escalations, overrides.

**Try it in ASI:One:** "login <passcode>" → "status" → "pin 12 to 2" → "wind 15 from 0".

Incident-command console for the wildfire rescue queue: live situation, per-station queues, escalations, and overrides that the dispatch policy must obey.

Part of a multi-agent system coordinating through a shared SpacetimeDB world state.
